# WagonWise terms for companies, with data processing: DRAFT for the solicitor (2026-11-26)

Not legal advice, and not published. Written at the owner's request to follow the shape of Tachomaster's terms (Road Tech Computer Systems
Ltd: [terms](https://www.tachomaster.co.uk/help/tandcp.php), [privacy policy](https://tachomaster.co.uk/privacypolicy/)), because it is the
same data. Anything in `[brackets]` is for the owner or the solicitor to decide. The solicitor should check the whole thing, above all
sections 4, 5 and 8.

**Where this differs from Tachomaster, on purpose:** it says outright that the company is the controller and WagonWise the processor
(their terms never say so; their separate processor notice does); it lists the sub-processors; it says what happens to data on ending
(theirs says only that you can have it, and deletes after 3 years); it promises no compliance, as theirs does not. Their liability cap is
10% of charges paid; ours is a bracket for the solicitor. The facts below match what the product does today (`modules.md`).

## 1. Who we are and what this covers

1.1 These terms are between WagonWise Ltd (company number 17530209, registered in England and Wales, registered office 5 Portland Terrace,
Hexham NE46 3DT) ("we") and the company that signs up for the WagonWise portal ("you"). They cover the portal, the jobs, checks,
maintenance, tachograph file and other tools in it, and the WagonWise driver app as your drivers use it for your work.

1.2 The driver app is free to drivers. A driver's own use of it, apart from your jobs, is covered by our
[privacy notice](https://wagon-wise.co.uk/privacy/) and is not part of these terms.

1.3 These terms and our privacy notice together are the whole agreement. A change we make to them applies from the date we tell you,
which is at least [30] days ahead when it is to your disadvantage.

## 2. Using the service

2.1 You choose who in your company has a portal account and what each may do. You must keep sign-in details secure and tell us at once of
any use you did not authorise. We are not responsible for loss caused by a sign-in you did not keep secure.

2.2 You are responsible for what you and the people you authorise put into the service and for how you use what it shows you.

2.3 **Trial.** A new company has a free trial of [the period agreed: about three months]. Trial data is not charged for. When the trial ends
we start charging as in section 6 unless you tell us you do not want to continue.

2.4 We aim to keep the service running but do not promise it is free of faults or interruptions. We may suspend or stop part of it for
maintenance or if charges are unpaid after we have written to you. We are not liable for an interruption, suspension or ending of the
service beyond what section 8 says.

2.5 **The service is a planning and record-keeping aid. It is not legal or compliance advice, and it does not make you compliant with
anything.** Routes, warnings, the driving-hours clock, check results and the tachograph tracker are guides. You remain responsible for your
legal duties as an operator, including drivers' hours, tachograph downloads and records, vehicle checks and maintenance, and for any
fine or penalty.

## 3. Your data

3.1 The data you put into the service, and the data your drivers create for you (jobs, positions during your jobs, walk-round checks,
proof of delivery, SOS alerts, tachograph files), **belongs to you**. We do not claim it.

3.2 We own the software, the website, the app and everything in them, including the intellectual property. You get the right to use them
for your business while these terms last.

3.3 We use the data only to provide the service to you, as section 4 says. We may use anonymous, combined figures that identify neither you
nor anyone else (for example how many companies use a screen) to improve the service.

## 4. Data protection

4.1 **Roles.** For personal data in the service that you put in or your drivers create for you, **you are the controller and we are your
processor**. You decide why and how it is used. For the driver app itself (a driver's own account, vehicle profiles, hazard reports and so
on) we are the controller, as our privacy notice says.

4.2 **What we process, and why.** On your written instructions, which are these terms and what you do in the portal: your staff's names,
emails and sign-in details; your drivers' names, sign-in identifiers and links to your company; jobs and stops; drivers' positions while on
your jobs (deleted after 30 days); walk-round checks and defect photos; proof-of-delivery photos and signatures; SOS alerts; driving-hours
status a driver chooses to share; and tachograph files. The purpose is to provide the service to you. It lasts as long as you use the
service and for the retention periods you choose.

4.3 **We will:**
(a) process the data only on your instructions, unless the law requires otherwise, in which case we will tell you first if we may;
(b) make sure the people who handle it are bound to keep it confidential;
(c) keep it secure (section 4.8);
(d) use only the sub-processors in 4.5 and tell you [30] days ahead of a change, so you may object;
(e) help you answer a driver's request about their data, and tell you promptly if a driver asks us directly about data we hold for you;
(f) help you with security, breach notices and impact assessments as far as the law needs, given what we hold;
(g) tell you **without undue delay, and [within 48 hours] of becoming aware**, of a breach affecting your data, with what we know;
(h) on request, give you the information needed to show we follow this section, and allow [one audit a year on reasonable notice, at
your cost];
(i) end and delete the data as in section 7.

4.4 **You will:** have a lawful basis for putting drivers' data into the service and for what you ask us to do with it; **tell your drivers
what you do with their data** (including that you hold their tachograph files and how long you keep them), for example in your own
employment privacy notice, because we cannot do that for you; and use the retention settings in the portal to keep data no longer than you
need.

4.5 **Sub-processors.** You agree we may use these, each only for what it needs, under written terms that give the same protection as this
section: DigitalOcean (hosting and database, London); Resend and ClickSend (emails and texts, including SOS emails to you);
what3words (turns a position into three words); the Met Office, National Highways, GOV.UK fuel prices and OpenStreetMap are open data we
fetch for everyone and receive none of your data. Others that handle some data when you use a feature: MapTiler, TomTom, DVSA, Anthropic,
Expo, Stripe (your payment details). **Tachograph files are held only by DigitalOcean** and go to no other service. [The solicitor to
confirm this list matches the privacy notice, section 5, at the time of signing.]

4.6 **Where it is held.** On servers in the UK. Where a sub-processor handles data outside the UK we use an approved transfer arrangement.

4.7 **Retention.** You choose how long checks, photos and tachograph files are kept (tachograph files never less than 12 months). Job
positions are deleted after 30 days. We delete a closed SOS alert after 30 days. Anything older than your setting is deleted for you.

4.8 **Security.** Everything sent between the app, the portal and our servers is encrypted. A company can see only its own data, enforced
in the database itself. Staff sign-in needs a second step. Passwords and tokens are stored hashed. Changes in the portal are logged. We
test and update these as the service changes.

## 5. Tachograph files

5.1 You can keep a copy of each driver card and vehicle unit download in the portal, from your drivers' phones or your own download tool.
We store each file **exactly as it was sent** with a checksum, return it unaltered, and **do not open it or work anything out from it**.

5.2 **You** are responsible for downloading drivers' cards and vehicle units as often as the law requires, for keeping the files and being
able to produce them, and for getting any company card and reader you need. The portal's tracker shows only the files sent to it and the
day each was said to be downloaded. A download not sent to the portal does not appear. It is a reminder, not a statement that you are
meeting your duties, and we accept no responsibility for a missed download, a fine or a penalty.

5.3 A driver can send you their own card file from the app only if you switch that on, and only after the app has told them it goes to
your company. You must handle it as in 4.4.

5.4 The firm decides how long files are kept. We delete a file when you delete it, or when it passes your retention period.

5.5 If a driver deletes their WagonWise account, the files they sent you stay with you as your record.

## 6. Charges

6.1 After the trial we charge [£10] per vehicle per month [founding rate; £15 standard], for each vehicle that is given a job or has a
walk-round check in that month, for the whole month. A vehicle first used in the last 7 days of a month is charged from the next month it
is used in. An idle vehicle costs nothing. Prices exclude VAT where VAT applies.

6.2 We invoice monthly, after the month ends, and collect by the card you give us through our payment provider, Stripe, or as the invoice
says. [Payment terms: the solicitor and the owner to set, for example 14 days.] We may change the price on [30] days' notice; the new price
applies from the next invoice after that.

6.3 If an invoice is unpaid after we have written to you, we may suspend your account after [14] days. Data is kept while the account is
suspended and for [30] days after, as section 7 says.

## 7. Ending, and your data

7.1 You may stop at any time by writing to support@wagon-wise.co.uk. [We may end these terms for a serious breach by you, or on [30] days'
notice.] Charges are owed up to the end of the month.

7.2 **On ending you may have all your data**: we will give you a copy of the jobs, checks, tachograph files and other records we hold for
you in a standard format within [30] days of your asking, free of charge.

7.3 **We delete it** [30] days after the account ends or after you have your copy, whichever is first, unless the law makes us keep
something. We will tell you if it does. Backups clear within [90] days.

## 8. Liability

8.1 Nothing limits liability for death or personal injury from negligence, for fraud, or for anything the law does not allow us to limit.

8.2 Subject to that, **our total liability to you in any year is limited to [the charges you paid us in the previous 12 months]** and we are
not liable for loss of profit, loss of business, fines or penalties, or indirect loss. [The solicitor to set the cap and say how data
protection claims fit. Tachomaster caps at 10% of charges paid; that is lower than most customers expect.]

8.3 We are not liable for loss caused by you or your people misusing the service or the data, by a sign-in kept insecurely, by a source
we do not control (such as a road closure feed, a traffic provider or a DVSA record being wrong or late), or by you relying on the service
for a legal duty (section 2.5).
