- 2026-11-25: **Billing on the vehicles used, not on the plan.** The owner's rule, to stop a firm having five vehicles and dropping to one just before the invoice: an invoice bills each vehicle that was **given a job or had a walk-round check** in the month, whole months at the firm's price, with nothing for an idle vehicle and a vehicle's **first week** (first use in the last 7 days of a month) left for the next month. It reads what was recorded, not the count on the day, and jobs and checks outlive a deleted vehicle. Invoices are now drafted for a **finished** month (`MonthNotFinished`). The plan's capacity stays as the limit on how many vehicles a firm can set up. The Plan page, admin Plans and Invoices pages and the For hauliers page say so. `docs/modules.md`, "Billing".
- 2026-11-24: **Portal home: a getting-started checklist for a new firm.** From the first-run review: a firm's first screen was "All clear: nothing needs you right now" above a wall of zeros. Now, while a firm has no vehicles and no jobs, the manager's home says "Welcome. Three short steps will get your fleet set up." and shows only a Getting started panel: add your lorry, add your drivers or yourself, create your first job, each ticked when done (a vehicle, an active driver link, a job), with a link to the guide. After the first vehicle the normal tiles and panels return above the panel; it disappears when all three are done. A step is only shown to someone who could do it (vehicles and drivers need manage vehicles & drivers, jobs need dispatch), and nothing is shown while a count is loading or has failed. Pure helper `lib/first-run.ts` with tests; tried in the portal on a local database. Dashboard only, no backend change.

- 2026-11-23: **Payments: the groundwork for the payment provider, with none connected (item 6 of the account-states plan).** A new `payments` module (migration 0104, `payments.customers`, `collections`, `processed_events`, all with Row-Level Security) holds everything around the provider that needs no account: a `PaymentProvider` port (the real Stripe adapter is a later, separate piece; `NotConfiguredPaymentProvider` is the default, so nothing is offered and every message is refused), payment details on file, "add payment details" on the plan page for a company's billing people (a hosted page at the provider; a paused company may use it), collecting an issued invoice by a button (one open attempt per invoice, so it cannot charge twice; nothing is collected by itself), and the webhook (`POST /webhooks/payments` on the driver server, passed to core's `POST /payments/webhook`). New email kind `resumed` ("active again"). `billing` gained three small methods for it. **Not done, needs the owner's Stripe account:** the real adapter, keys, and a test-mode run. Built on the account-states branch (PR 429), opened against `main`.

- 2026-11-23: **Company account states: the automatic rules (item 3).** An hourly task (`company-account-rules`, `companies.runAccountRules`) now does what the scope’s loop describes, behind a new setting `companies.account_settings.automation` (migration 0101): `off` (how it starts: nothing happens by itself), `no_pause` (emails, and a finished free period becomes overdue, but nothing is ever paused) or `full` (an overdue company is paused once the grace period has passed). The decision is a pure function, `nextAction` in `domain/account-rules.ts`: a trial gets the 14 and 7 day emails and becomes overdue the **day after its last free day** (there is no payment provider, so nobody has payment details on file); an overdue company gets the overdue email, a reminder a week in, and in `full` mode is paused when `graceDays` have passed; a company with the owner’s hold, an active one and a paused one are left alone, and a pause made by hand is not emailed. Nothing is done before 7am UK. `runAccountRules` (`application/run-account-rules.ts`) works one company at a time in its own platform-staff data scope, so one failure does not stop the rest; after a move it asks again, so a company just made overdue is written to in the same pass and one just paused gets the paused email. Each automatic move is logged with no staff member (shown as "by the rules"), and the owner is sent a notice (`ALERT_EMAIL`). The overdue email promises a pause day only in `full` mode and when there is no hold. Admin Companies page: an "Automatic rules" select (switching to `full` asks for confirmation once), a "Run the rules now" button (`POST /staff/account-rules/run`, WagonWise staff only: the same pass, so it still waits for 7am and does nothing while off), and a History list under each company’s Account editor (`GET /staff/companies/:id/account-history`: moves, by hand or by the rules, and emails sent). **It ships switched off:** the owner turns it on once.
