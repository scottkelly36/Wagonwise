Questions

Common questions

Do I need to fit anything in my lorries?

No. There is nothing to fit. It runs on the phones your drivers already have.

What phones do my drivers need?

The app is in testing on Android now. An iPhone version is planned, and the Google Play and App Store releases are coming. Register your firm and we will tell you when there is a place for you.

Can I see where my drivers are all the time?

No. A driver’s position is shared with you only while they are on one of your jobs and the app is open. The app never tracks in the background, and a driver can delete their own account in the app.

Does WagonWise make us compliant?

No. It helps you keep checks, defects and maintenance dates in one place, but you stay responsible for your legal duties. The driving-hours clock in the app is a guide: the tachograph is the legal record.

What does it cost?

The driver app is free. The fleet dashboard is £15 per vehicle per month. Founding firms get three free months, then £10 per vehicle per month for their first year.

Where does the routing work?

At the moment routing covers Northumberland, Tyne and Wear and Cumbria. We add more of Great Britain when a firm needs it. Northern Ireland is not covered.
