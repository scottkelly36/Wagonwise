Privacy notice

1. Who we are

WagonWise is run by Scott Kelly. It provides a route planning and job app for HGV drivers, and a web portal for the companies they drive for. You can contact us about your data at support@wagon-wise.co.uk.

We are in two roles:

  • We are the controller (we decide how your data is used) for the driver app itself: your sign-in, your vehicle profiles and routes, hazard reports, and feedback.
  • We are the processor (we handle data on a company's instructions) for the job information a company puts into the portal, and where a driver is while doing one of that company's jobs. That company is the controller.

3. Your location

We take location seriously because it is the most personal thing the app handles.

  • The app only uses your location while it is open on your screen. It does not track you in the background.
  • While the app is open, it sends your position (or the middle of the part of the map you are looking at, if you have moved the map away from you) to our servers so it can show hazards, traffic reports and parking there, and weather warnings for your area (rounded to about 10 km). We use it as it arrives and do not store it.
  • While you are on a trip, it also sends your position so we can warn you about hazards ahead, notice if you leave the route, and suggest a faster one. We do not store it. Road locations rounded to about 500 metres go to TomTom for live traffic.
  • Your position is kept, and shown to a company, only while you are on one of that company's jobs that is on the road. Those positions are deleted after 30 days. The company cannot see where you are at any other time.
  • When you file a hazard, traffic or parking report, the place of the report is saved with it, without your name.
  • If you drive for a company, that company can see where you are during its jobs. Your employer or contractor should tell you about this too.

4. Why we are allowed to use your data

  • To provide the app you asked for (signing in, planning routes, warning you about hazards): it is needed to do what you signed up for.
  • For company jobs: the company relies on its own lawful basis, usually that it has a legitimate interest in knowing where its vehicles and goods are, balanced against your rights. We act on its instructions.
  • For location and hazard reports: you agree when you first open the app, and you can say no or delete your account.
  • To keep the service safe and working (logs, fixing faults): our legitimate interest in running a secure service.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Some of these services may handle data outside the UK. Where they do, we use an approved transfer arrangement, such as the UK's adequacy regulations or the UK addendum to the standard contractual clauses.

6. How we keep it safe

  • Everything sent between the app and our servers is encrypted.
  • Companies can only see their own data. This is enforced in the database itself, not only in the app.
  • Sign-in tokens last 15 minutes. Passwords are stored scrambled and refresh tokens are stored hashed.
  • Company staff must use a second step (an authenticator app) to sign in.
  • Changes made in the portal are logged.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • show you the data we hold about you,
  • correct it,
  • delete it,
  • stop using it in a particular way, or give it to you in a usable form.

To delete your account: open the app, go to More, and choose Delete account. This removes your sign-in details, name, vehicle profiles, routes, trips and feedback, signs you out everywhere and removes the phone from our list. Hazard, traffic and parking reports you made stay, with nothing linking them to you. See also how to delete your account. For the other rights, or if you can't use the app, email support@wagon-wise.co.uk and we will answer within one month.

If you drive for a company, a request about job data may need to go through that company, because it controls that data. We will tell you if that is the case.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113). We would like the chance to put it right first.

8. Safety

WagonWise is a planning aid. It does not replace road signs, official restrictions or your own judgement. Hazards reported by other drivers can only make a route more cautious. They never override a restriction or sign you can see on the road. Faster-route suggestions are only offers: they never change your route unless you choose to switch.
