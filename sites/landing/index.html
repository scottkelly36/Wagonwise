WagonWise

A driver app built for HGVs, and a dashboard for the firm that runs them.

We are getting ready to test it with real drivers and small fleets. Put your email down and we will tell you when there is a place for you.

What it does

  • Routes that suit your vehicle’s height, weight and length, with hazards other drivers have reported.
  • Daily vehicle checks on the phone, with defects and photos going straight to the office.
  • Jobs sent to the driver, with the delivery record kept.
  • A driver-hours clock that helps plan breaks.
  • For the firm: live map, maintenance reminders, reports, and what each job cost and made.

It is still in testing, so things will change. Testers get it free and tell us what is wrong with it.
