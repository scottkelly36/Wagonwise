The driver app

Everything a driver needs on one screen

Built with and for HGV drivers. It is free to use, and it is a planning aid: road signs and your own judgement always come first.

Routes

Routes that fit your vehicle

Tell WagonWise your height, width, length and weight once. It plans routes that keep your vehicle clear of bridges and roads it cannot take, and shows the distance, time and fuel cost before you set off.

  • Your vehicle’s measurements used on every route
  • Time, distance and a rough fuel cost up front
  • Spoken turn-by-turn directions, with a turn card on screen
Hazards

Hazards before you reach them

Low bridges, tight bends, roadworks and flooding reported by other drivers show up on your route, with a spoken warning as you approach. Reports are anonymous, and they only ever make a route more cautious.

  • See what is on your route before you set off
  • Spoken warnings, so your eyes stay on the road
  • A report never overrides a sign you can see
Parking

Safe parking, at a glance

Find HGV parks, services and spots other drivers have recommended. Green shows what is there: toilets, showers, shop, food, fuel, lighting, security. Grey means it is not there, or nobody has said yet.

  • How many HGVs a site holds, and whether it is free or paid
  • One tap to navigate there
  • Mark a spot yourself so the next driver finds it

Some sites are small and can fill up. We show how many HGVs a site holds, not how many spaces are free right now, so have a back-up in mind.

Traffic

A quicker way, when traffic builds

While you drive, WagonWise looks for a clearly faster route using live traffic and reports from other drivers. It only offers an alternative route, and you choose whether to switch or carry on. It never changes your route by itself.

  • Only offered when it really saves time
  • Always a route that suits your vehicle
  • Switch with one tap, or keep going
Reporting

Warn the next driver in seconds

Tap a hazard type on the map, or report by voice while you drive. Your report reaches other drivers without your name on it.

  • Low bridge, weight limit, width limit, tight bend and more
  • Hands-free voice reports
  • Traffic and parking reports too
Jobs

Jobs from your firm, on your phone

Your firm sends you jobs with the stops, notes and gate details. Update the status as you go and take a proof-of-delivery photo when one is needed. Your firm sees your position only while a job is on the road and the app is open.

  • Stops, times and notes in one place
  • Mark gates and entrances for the next driver
  • Daily vehicle checks and a driving-hours clock

Questions

Common questions

What is WagonWise?

WagonWise is a navigation app for HGV drivers in Great Britain. You tell it your vehicle's height, width, length and weight once, and it plans routes that keep to roads your vehicle can use. It also shows warnings from other drivers about low bridges, tight bends and other hazards, safe lorry parking, and jobs from your firm.

Is WagonWise free?

The driver app is free for drivers, and testers get everything free. Firms that use the fleet dashboard pay per vehicle: get in touch for details.

Which phones does it run on?

Android is in testing now. An iPhone version is planned. Register to test and we will tell you when there is a place for you.

Where does it work?

Routing covers Great Britain: England, Scotland and Wales. Northern Ireland is not covered.

Does it replace road signs and my own judgement?

No. WagonWise is a planning aid. Always follow road signs, the law and your own judgement.

Does it track me?

Your location is used only while the app is open. It is shared with your firm only while you are on one of its jobs. Hazard, parking and traffic reports never show your name, and you can delete your account in the app at any time.

Does a haulage firm need any equipment to use the fleet dashboard?

No. There is nothing to fit. It runs on the phones your drivers already have.
